# ADVENTURE-WORKS-POWER-BI-PROJECT
Power BI dashboard analyzing Adventure Works sales, budget, and tax performance (2014–2017) — with category, geography, and top product/customer breakdowns.

The KPIs are:
Total Budget: 17M
Total Sales: $29.31M
Total Tax: $2.34M

Features
- Year filter (2014–2017)
- Budget vs. Sales by category, country, and month
- Total Tax by year
- Top 5 products & customers

 Key Insight
Bikes dominate sales and budget; Accessories/Clothing are underdeveloped. US & Australia drive most sales, while France and Canada lag. Tax spiked notably in 2016.
